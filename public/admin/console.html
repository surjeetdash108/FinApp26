Publishing an HTML article

  1. Choose HTML, then download Preset 1 · Article from the Design row below.
  2. Replace its text and images with yours. Keep the layout and class names as they are.
  3. Bring it back with Load .html file, then fill Title, Summary and Hero image — all three are required.
  4. Preview at each device width. Fix whatever it flags, then Publish; it goes live at once.
Three things that will break your post
  • JavaScript never runs — not in the preview, not on the site. Anything a script draws (tabs, carousels, counters, charts, post lists) publishes empty. Write it as plain HTML.
  • Off-site fonts, stylesheets and url() images are dropped by the site’s security policy. Use a system font stack and upload your images. Tailwind classes do work — the site serves its own copy.
  • It must hold up from 360px. Size with max-width, never a fixed width:1200px; use repeat(auto-fit,minmax(240px,1fr)) over repeat(4,300px); give card rows flex-wrap:wrap. The preview measures this and tells you, in pixels, when it does not fit.

Your <style> is saved with this post only — a later upload will not restyle it. On save the site drops your own masthead and any “Back to blogs” link, because it draws both itself; it also uploads base64 images for you and gives wide tables their own sideways scroll. Unclosed tags are caught before publishing, with line numbers. Limits: hero 5 MB, PDF/Word 20 MB. Preset 2 is the blog-index design, not an article.

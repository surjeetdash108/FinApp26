NVIDIA clears $96 billion in a quarter, and guides to $108 billion

Revenue doubled year over year and gross margin held at 75%. The line worth your attention sits further down the cash flow statement.

NVIDIA reported second-quarter fiscal 2027 results on 26 August, for the quarter ended 26 July 2026. Revenue of $96.22 billion was up 18% sequentially and 106% year over year, and gross margin held at 75.0% on both a GAAP and non-GAAP basis.

The quarter in one line

The company added $14.6 billion of revenue sequentially and $49.5 billion year over year. Data centre revenue of $89.0 billion made up roughly 92% of the total, rising 18% quarter over quarter and 117% year over year. Edge Computing — the PC, robotics and automotive stack — contributed $7.2 billion.

Management guided third-quarter revenue to $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%, another step of $11.8 billion, and stated that the guide assumes no data centre compute revenue from China.

Why GAAP earnings came in above non-GAAP

GAAP diluted EPS of $2.46 sits above the non-GAAP figure of $2.22. The gap is $7.77 billion of net gains on equity securities, which the non-GAAP measure strips out.

Worth flagging. From the first quarter of fiscal 2027, NVIDIA stopped excluding stock-based compensation from its non-GAAP measures and restated history to match. Non-GAAP comparisons against pre-FY27 prints as originally published are not like for like.

The line to watch is in the cash flow statement

Operating cash flow was $24.1 billion against $59.7 billion of net income. Receivables rose $22.3 billion in the quarter and inventories $5.8 billion — consistent with a build ahead of the Rubin ramp, and the single line worth checking first next quarter.

  • Roughly $26.0 billion was returned to shareholders through buybacks and dividends, with about $99.0 billion left on the repurchase authorisation.
  • The next dividend of $0.25 per share is payable 1 October 2026 to holders of record on 10 September.
  • Long-term debt rose to $32.4 billion from $7.5 billion at the January year-end, following a $24.9 billion raise.

What management pointed at

The Vera Rubin platform is in full production, with racks running at CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and Nebius. The company also announced financing partnerships intended to mobilise more than $500 billion of third-party capital for AI infrastructure, subject to definitive agreements.

What this means for a portfolio

At this size the beat is largely assumed and the guide carries the argument, which is one reason a strong print can still be met with a soft tape. The concentration cuts both ways: data centre at 92% of revenue is why the top line doubled, and it is why a single quarter of hyperscaler digestion would show up across the whole P&L.

FAQs

Which quarter did NVIDIA just report?

Fiscal second quarter 2027, covering the three months ended 26 July 2026, reported after the close on 26 August 2026.

Why is fiscal 2027 the same as calendar 2026?

NVIDIA's fiscal year ends in late January, so the year labelled fiscal 2027 runs mostly through calendar 2026.

What is the guidance for next quarter?

Revenue of $108.0 billion plus or minus 2%, gross margin of 74.0% plus or minus 50 basis points, and GAAP operating expenses of roughly $9.2 billion. The guide assumes no data centre compute revenue from China.

Why did free cash flow fall against the prior quarter?

Working capital absorbed most of the earnings. Receivables rose $22.3 billion and inventories $5.8 billion, leaving operating cash flow of $24.1 billion against $59.7 billion of net income.
